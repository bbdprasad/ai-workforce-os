# Problem

## Working problem statement

Enterprise AI adoption can create a new operational problem: organizations may deploy agents across departments, vendors, frameworks, and systems without a neutral operational model for understanding the resulting workforce.

Potential consequences include:

- unknown agent inventory;
- unclear ownership and accountability;
- excessive or inappropriate permissions;
- inconsistent evaluation;
- difficult incident investigation;
- unclear cost attribution; and
- inability to connect agent activity to business outcomes.

These are hypotheses to validate through customer discovery and experiments.

## Questions to answer

1. Is agent sprawl actually painful enough to buy software for?
2. Who owns the problem: CIO, CTO, CISO, platform engineering, AI engineering, or business operations?
3. What is the first painful workflow?
4. What existing products already solve most of it?
5. What cannot be solved by the major cloud/model vendors?
6. What evidence would make us kill this thesis?
