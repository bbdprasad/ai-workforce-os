# Vision

AI changes the composition of work.

The long-term vision is a company operating layer in which humans and AI workers are represented as first-class organizational entities.

An enterprise should eventually be able to understand:

- who or what is doing work;
- what role it has;
- what it is allowed to access;
- what it is responsible for;
- which humans supervise it;
- which agents it collaborates with;
- how reliably it performs;
- what it costs; and
- what measurable business value it creates.

The vision is intentionally broader than the first product. The first product must earn the right to expand into this platform.
